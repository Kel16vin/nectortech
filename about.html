About Nector

Built on precision, backed by experience.

A Uganda-based engineering and construction firm delivering integrated technical solutions for the built environment — from the drawing board to the final walk-through.

Who we are

One team, every discipline a project needs.

Nector Technical Services is based in Kyengera, at Naviga Plaza, and works across construction, civil, electrical and mechanical engineering, and real estate development. Rather than handing clients off between separate contractors, we keep design, delivery and project oversight under one accountable roof.

That means a client planning a building doesn't just get a contractor — they get architectural and structural input, surveying, project management, and the electrical and plumbing works that make a structure livable, coordinated from day one.

Our mission is to deliver durable, high-quality engineering and construction solutions that clients can trust — and our vision is to be a technical services partner Uganda's built environment can rely on for the long term.

HOW A PROJECT MOVES · 01–04
01

Consult

We visit the site or review the brief, understand constraints and budget, and agree the real scope before any drawing starts.

02

Design

Architectural, structural, electrical and mechanical drawings are developed together, so nothing is redesigned mid-build.

03

Build

Construction, surveying and installation work proceeds under active project management, with safety and quality checks at each stage.

04

Handover

Clients receive a completed, inspected structure with documentation — and a team that remains reachable after handover.
